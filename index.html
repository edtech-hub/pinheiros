Questions people ask

Which areas do you cover?

Palm Beach County and Broward. We’re based in Deerfield Beach. If you’re not sure we come out your way, call and ask.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. The team brings its own equipment and products.

What if I want something cleaned again?

Tell the team before they leave. They ask you to check the place, and they’ll go back over anything you point out.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Ask us for the details when you book.

Do you clean offices and other businesses?

Yes. Offices, restaurants, medical spaces and sites after construction, as well as homes.

Can you turn over a vacation rental between guests?

Yes. We clean rental and vacation homes so they’re ready when the next guests arrive.
